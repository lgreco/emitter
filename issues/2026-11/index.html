From the Desk of the President

Friends, hams, countrymen: lend me your ears, and also your coax.

October was the month the club discovered that a silent auction is only silent until two members both want the same oscilloscope. We raised an amount of money best described as "a lot, in the way a bucket is a lot of water." Thank you to every donor, bidder, and the gentleman who tried to bid on the folding table he had donated.

Public service report: the great cone count

Our volunteers covered the course with distinction and only one unscheduled sprint. Highlights from the after-action report:

  • Cups of coffee consumed per operator: 3.7
  • Times someone said "Net control, say again": 41
  • Number of bananas eaten: classified

Radio operators, as always, were the calm center of a very loud storm. Thank you to everyone who stood in the cold so that others could run past in shorts.

Gadget of the Month: the Left-Handed Smoke Detector

This month's gadget is a device that detects only left-handed smoke. It has no display, no buttons, and no known use, which makes it the most reliable item in the shack. Our reviewer rates it "four out of five solder blobs" and notes that it pairs well with the Gadget of Last Month, which we have misplaced.

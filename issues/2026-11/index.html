October was a busy month for the club. Thank you to everyone who donated, bid, and volunteered at the October 13 auction, and to everyone who helped with the Chicago Marathon on October 11. The online sale of donated items is still open, with new items being added.

Next up is our November meeting on Tuesday, November 10, with an Elecraft product review from Eric Swartz. In December, on Tuesday, December 8, the club elects its board for 2027. If you have thought about helping run the club, now is the time to say so. We are looking for people for Public Relations, Website and Newsletter Authors, and Voluntary Examiners. If you have not been to the Saturday breakfast at Max's, come try it. We are there every Saturday at 8 am, and recent turnouts have been 23 to 28 people.

"See" you on the bands, 73.

Online sale of donated equipment

The club's online sale of donated items continues throughout the year. Browse the list and make an offer on the auction page: https://ns9rc.org/nsrc-auction.

  • An offer below the listed price stays open for two weeks.
  • If no higher offer arrives, an offer of at least 50% of fair market value wins the item.
  • Won items are picked up at the Deerfield storage locker; details are sent by email.
